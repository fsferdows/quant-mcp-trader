# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and honest technical feedback
- Focusing on reproducible evidence and responsible risk disclosure in trading research

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, trolling, insulting comments, or personal attacks
- Publishing misleading financial claims, fabricated backtests, or guaranteed-profit promises
- Soliciting private API keys, credentials, or personal information

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainer (**FS Ferdows**) at `fsferdows@icloud.com`.
